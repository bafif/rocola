What it is

Boot an old PC from the stick and a full-screen jukebox comes up, ready to play. When you decide to, a guided installer copies it to the internal disk: from then on the PC starts straight into the jukebox, driven with arcade controls, on a modern monitor or a CRT. It plays through analog, HDMI, USB or Bluetooth speakers and speaks English and Spanish.

Why Rocola

  • A1

    Try it without touching the disk

    The live system runs in RAM on top of the stick's read-only image: nothing is written to the PC until you install.

  • A2

    It never wipes a disk on its own

    The installer opens only from the menu, and only from the stick; you pick the disk and type a confirmation word. Reinstalling can keep the music.

  • A3

    Made for old PCs

    Debian i386 with a PAE kernel and a non-PAE fallback, about 2 GB of RAM, and boot over BIOS (32- and 64-bit PCs) or UEFI x64.

  • A4

    Arcade controls with no setup

    Encoders that show up as a keyboard or as a joystick work at the same time, and «Test controls» shows what each button sends.

  • A5

    CRTs too

    VGA monitors and 15 kHz arcade or TV screens, with fixed modelines; the monitor type is chosen once, when installing. 15 kHz depends on the video card and its driver.

  • A6

    Music that stays

    Preloaded in the image, or copied from a second USB stick as soon as you plug it in. Once installed, it lives in a /music partition that survives reinstalls.

Install

You need a USB stick of 1 GB or more (it gets erased).

  1. Download rocola-i386-<version>.iso (about 671 MiB) and its SHA256SUMS.txt from the latest release.
  2. Write it to the stick:
    ToolHow
    Rufus (Windows)«Write in DD Image mode» (step by step)
    Ventoyinstall Ventoy on the stick once, then copy the .iso onto it
    balenaEtcher«Flash from file», the .iso, the stick, «Flash»
    dd (Linux)sudo dd if=rocola-i386-<version>.iso of=/dev/sdX bs=4M conv=fsync

On a UEFI PC, turn Secure Boot off: the boot loader isn't signed. Checking the download and the stick, and trying it in QEMU: Flashing the USB stick.

How to use it

  1. 1

    Boot the old PC from the stick

    From its boot menu or by changing the boot order. «Rocola — English» starts it in English; «Rocola», in Spanish.

  2. 2

    Play music

    Plug in a second stick with songs: they are copied and show up in the library. The arrows and Enter move and pick, F10 opens the menu, and the arcade buttons are in Arcade controls.

  3. 3

    Install it

    Plug in a keyboard, open the menu and choose «Install to disk». Pick the language and the disk (14 GB or more), type the confirmation word and pick the monitor type. Remove the stick and reboot: from then on the PC starts straight into the jukebox.

Status

Beta. In QEMU/KVM it works end to end: it boots to the jukebox over BIOS and UEFI x64, the installer clones it to an empty disk that then boots on its own, songs from a second stick show up in the library, and reinstalling keeps the music.

On real hardware the live system boots from a Ventoy stick. Not tried there yet: 15 kHz on a CRT, an arcade encoder, importing from a second stick and reinstalling while keeping the music.

All the detail is in the documentation.
